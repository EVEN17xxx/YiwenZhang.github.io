Investigated how government size and legal frameworks influence economic freedom using the 2016 Human Freedom Index dataset. Applied Principal Component Analysis to reduce dimensionality and identify key drivers of freedom metrics, followed by hierarchical clustering to group 162 countries into meaningful economic categories, using `missMDA` to handle missing values robustly.
